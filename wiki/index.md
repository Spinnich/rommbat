# RomMBat

RomMBat keeps a [RetroBat](https://www.retrobat.org/) install on Windows in step with your
self-hosted [RomM](https://github.com/rommapp/romm) library. You pick which games you want on
this machine, and RomMBat brings them down with their artwork, metadata and BIOS. When you play,
it sends your saves, save states and playtime back to RomM, so every device you play on sees
the same progress.

RomM stays in charge of your library, and RetroBat is where you play it. Everything RomMBat
needs lives inside the RetroBat folder, so a RetroBat on a USB stick keeps working after you
move it to another PC.

!!! warning "RomMBat is not released yet"

    This guide is being written alongside the first release. Until it is complete, the
    [project README](https://github.com/Spinnich/rommbat#readme) says what works today, which
    versions of RomM and RetroBat you need, and which systems have been tested.
